barebones aimbot mod that activates at the press of M2 (only works over range with the AR or Sniper as those are hitscan. I designed the mod around this due to the sniper being the best for high mult trickshots.) Works best when firing at the apex of a jump to mitigate the sway caused by jumping (providing you want to go for arial shots).



V1.0.! Changes: now tracks crabs, lobsters, and the albatross. (extended tracking range specifically for the albatross to make sure the lock on occurs even when its flying 3 business days away)



cleaned up the code to make it as lightweight as possible while still remaining functional.
