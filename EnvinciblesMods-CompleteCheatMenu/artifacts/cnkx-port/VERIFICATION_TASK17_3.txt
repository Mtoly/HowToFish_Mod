- InitialVelocityRedirector preserves each projectile's original speed magnitude and replaces only direction.

- Created local projectiles are registered to ProjectileTracker with the shot's fixed TargetSolution.
- Spawn matching requires the active weapon holder, active WeaponInfo, isLocal=true, non-NPC origin, and a still-valid target.
- In-flight guidance and removal-queue cleanup are not implemented in this stage.
