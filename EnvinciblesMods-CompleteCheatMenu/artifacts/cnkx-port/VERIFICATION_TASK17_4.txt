CHANGED_BRANCH_OR_FIELD
- UpdateProjectileScan Prefix runs guidance before the game's projectile collision scan.
- ProjectileGuidance uses only the TargetSolution registered at shot creation and never reacquires or switches targets in flight.
- Per-projectile state stores StartTime, NextUpdate, target identity, and the aim-point offset from the live target transform.
- Target velocity prefers Rigidbody.GetPointVelocity and falls back to the bounded VelocityTracker.
- ProjectileGuidanceMath preserves projectile speed while applying a configurable degrees-per-second turn cap.
- PredictProjectile uses Projectile.GravityForce, three iterations, and a configurable maximum prediction time.
- Initial velocity injection now uses WeaponInfo.ProjectileGravity instead of generic Physics gravity.
- Tracking duration, update interval, turn speed, and max prediction time are persisted and exposed in the Chinese weapon UI.
- Tracking duration zero leaves Task 17.3 initial redirection only; turn speed zero performs immediate steering.
- Target loss or timeout keeps the projectile's last velocity and unregisters guidance without selecting another target.
- AddToRemoveQueue Postfix clears ProjectileTracker and ProjectileGuidance state.
- Disabling aimbot stops velocity writes and forgets active guidance state.
