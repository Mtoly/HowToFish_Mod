# SeaLegs

![](https://cdn.hexium.gg/upload/699/images/39b4b27aa80894f1fc5a5cc2.png)

Everyone else sees your whole body, why shouldn't you? The game deletes all of it the moment you spawn and leaves you a
pair of floating hands. This gives it back, exactly the way everyone else sees you.

No head, though. The camera sits inside your skull, so you'd be staring at the back of your own face. It gets folded
away just for you, and your shadow keeps its head.

Client side only.

I tested this very heavy in singleplayer. Lightly in multiplayer. Please report bugs!

## Config

Everything defaults to something sane. Worth knowing about:

- **Body Offset** — how far the body sits from the camera. 20 cm back by default, which keeps you out of your own
  collar when you look down.
- **Components To Enable** — advanced. The list of components the game switches off on your own player that get turned
  back on. Don't touch it unless something's broken and you know what you're changing.

## Installation

Drop `SeaLegs.dll` in `BepInEx/plugins`.
